# DataRelay Labs

**Source-available products for secure connectivity, controlled data movement, and enterprise data delivery.**

DataRelay Labs is the common home for independent products that solve practical connectivity and data-delivery problems. Each product keeps its own runtime, documentation, release lifecycle, and operational boundary.

<Note>
  Start with the problem you need to solve. DataRelay Link and DataRelay Control are separate products; neither one is required to use the other.
</Note>

## Current products

## Built to grow as a product family

`datarelay.run` is the DataRelay Labs organization portal. Individual products live on their own product sites, such as `link.datarelay.run` and `control.datarelay.run`. Future DataRelay Labs products can be added here without turning the existing products into components of one bundled platform.
