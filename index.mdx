**Source-available products for secure connectivity, controlled data movement, enterprise data delivery, and human-approved automation.**

DataRelay Labs brings together independent products for secure connectivity, data delivery, and approval-controlled automation. Each product has its own runtime, documentation, releases, and clearly defined responsibility.

## Current products

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="DataRelay Link" icon="link" href="https://link.datarelay.run/">

## Built to grow as a product family

`datarelay.run` is the DataRelay Labs organization portal. Individual products live on their own product sites, such as `link.datarelay.run`, `control.datarelay.run`, and `grant.datarelay.run`. Future DataRelay Labs products can be added here without turning the existing products into components of one bundled platform.
