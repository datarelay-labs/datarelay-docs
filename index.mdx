# DataRelay Labs

**Source-available products for secure connectivity, controlled data movement, enterprise data delivery, and approval-gated automation.**

DataRelay Labs is the common home for independent products that solve practical connectivity, data-delivery, and execution-governance problems. Each product keeps its own runtime, documentation, release lifecycle, and operational boundary.

<Note>
  Start with the problem you need to solve. DataRelay Link, DataRelay Control, and DataRelay Grant are separate products. Grant can complement the other products as an approval layer, but none is required to use another.
</Note>

## Current products

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="DataRelay Link" icon="link" href="https://link.datarelay.run/">
    Lightweight secure connectivity for isolated and restricted networks: Remote Access to approved internal Remote Services, Internet Access to approved external destinations, and AI Access for authenticated AI identities with explicit permissions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DataRelay Control" icon="sliders" href="https://control.datarelay.run/">
    Enterprise Data Control Gateway using a **One Stream → Many Routes → Many Destinations** model for collection, route-specific processing, governance, protection, and delivery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DataRelay Grant" icon="shield-check" href="https://github.com/datarelay-labs/datarelay-grant-docs">
    Coming-soon approval and execution-control layer based on **US 12,056,667 B1** and **KR 10-2567118 B1**. It is designed to place an explicit human decision between a requested action and its execution.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Built to grow as a product family

`datarelay.run` is the DataRelay Labs organization portal. Individual products live on their own product sites or repositories, such as `link.datarelay.run`, `control.datarelay.run`, and the current DataRelay Grant documentation repository. Future DataRelay Labs products can be added here without turning the existing products into components of one bundled platform.
